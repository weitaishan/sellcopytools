Amazon listing copy basics

Amazon explains that a product detail page includes the product name, images, description, and other listing information. The description gives a broad overview of the product and its benefits, while bullet points highlight the most important details and attributes. Your copy should make the product category, main benefit, and strongest differentiators obvious without feeling stuffed with keywords.

  • Name the product type clearly.
  • Lead with the strongest customer outcome.
  • Use bullets for the most important features.
  • Include practical details like size, material, quantity, or compatibility.
  • Avoid unsupported performance, health, or safety claims.

Five bullet point angles to generate

Amazon shoppers often scan bullet points before they read the full description. Generate bullets that each answer a different buyer question.

Benefit

What daily problem does the product solve?

Specification

What size, material, quantity, or compatibility detail matters?

Use case

Where or when will shoppers use the product?

Included items

What comes in the box, bundle, or package?

Trust detail

What care, warranty, setup, or support note reduces hesitation?

Better inputs create better listing drafts

Before generating copy, collect product details that buyers care about: use case, audience, problem solved, included items, dimensions, warranty, care instructions, and comparison points. These details make the draft more useful and reduce editing time.

Useful Amazon copy angles

Try angles like durability, convenience, giftability, everyday use, space saving, premium materials, easy setup, or compatibility. Choose one primary angle per listing so the message stays focused.

FAQ

What should an Amazon product description include?

Include what the product is, who it is for, the main benefit, important specifications, included items, and practical use cases. Keep every claim tied to the real product.

Should Amazon copy include bullet points?

Yes. Use bullet points to highlight important product details and attributes such as material, dimensions, compatibility, package contents, and buyer objections.

Can I publish generated Amazon copy without editing?

No. Treat generated copy as a draft. Check product facts, category rules, restricted claims, and marketplace policy before publishing.
