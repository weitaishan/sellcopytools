What Amazon bullets should do

Amazon shoppers often compare multiple listings quickly. Bullets should surface the reasons to choose your product: fit, durability, ease of use, included items, compatibility, and practical outcomes.

  • Lead each bullet with a clear benefit.
  • Support benefits with concrete product details.
  • Keep claims accurate and policy-safe.
  • Answer common buyer questions before they become objections.
  • Make every bullet distinct instead of repeating the same promise.

Feature-to-benefit structure

A useful structure is: benefit, feature, use case. For example, “Stay organized on busy mornings with a divided interior that keeps keys, cards, and cables easy to find.” This gives both proof and context.

Amazon bullet point examples

Kitchen product

Cook with less sticking and easier cleanup thanks to a smooth ceramic coating designed for everyday eggs, vegetables, and quick meals.

Desk accessory

Keep your workspace tidy with divided sections for keys, cards, earbuds, and charging cables.

Travel item

Pack faster for weekend trips with lightweight compartments that separate toiletries, chargers, and small essentials.

Character limit and guidelines

Amazon bullet rules can vary by category and may change over time. Treat generated copy as a draft, then confirm the current category requirements in Seller Central before publishing. A practical working range for many listings is to keep bullets scannable, specific, and free of unsupported claims.

  • Do not include pricing, promotions, coupons, or shipping promises unless the marketplace explicitly allows them.
  • Avoid medical, safety, or performance claims you cannot prove.
  • Use the product's real materials, dimensions, compatibility, and included parts.
  • Write for shoppers first and search second.

Before publishing

Review marketplace rules, category requirements, restricted claims, and actual product specs. Generated copy should be edited before it becomes live listing content.

FAQ

How many bullet points should an Amazon listing have?

Many Amazon product detail pages use up to five bullet points for key product features. Use each one for a different buyer question instead of repeating the same benefit.

What makes a good Amazon bullet point?

Lead with a clear benefit, support it with concrete product details, and keep the claim accurate. The best bullets help shoppers compare quickly.

Should Amazon bullet points include keywords?

Yes, but naturally. Add search terms where they help explain the product. Avoid stuffing repeated or irrelevant keywords.
